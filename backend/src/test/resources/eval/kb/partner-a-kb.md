# Partner A (North Goa beach hostel) — Official Rules

- **Check-in & Check-out**: Standard check-in begins at 1:00 PM. Check-out is by 10:30 AM sharp. Valid government ID (Passport for foreigners, Aadhaar/Voter ID for Indians) required at check-in. PAN card is NOT accepted.
- **Rooms & Rates**: 8-bed mixed dorm is ₹599/night. 6-bed mixed AC dorm is ₹699/night. 4-bed AC private ensuite is ₹2400/night.
- **Breakfast & Cafe**: Complimentary chai and toast from 8:30 AM to 10:30 AM on the rooftop. In-house cafe serves Goan curry, sandwiches, and smoothies until 11:30 PM.
- **Policies**: Curfew for main gate is 1:00 AM for safety. Rooftop chill zone stays open 24/7 with quiet hours after midnight. Outside drinks allowed with ₹100 corkage per bottle. Strictly no drugs or contraband (zero tolerance policy).
- **Facilities**: Coin laundry available for ₹150 per wash load. Secure lockers provided in all dorms (bring your own padlock or buy at desk for ₹100).
- **Escalation**: For room switches, extended stays, or lost keys, speak with the front desk manager.

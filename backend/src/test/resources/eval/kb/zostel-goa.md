# Zostel Goa — House Rules & Information

- **Check-in / Check-out**: Check-in is from 2:00 PM to 11:00 PM. Check-out is strictly by 11:00 AM. Late check-out after 11:00 AM incurs a half-day charge.
- **Pricing**: 6-bed mixed AC dorm is ₹650 per night. 4-bed female AC dorm is ₹750 per night. All rates include GST.
- **Amenities**: Free high-speed Wi-Fi (Network: Zostel_Guest, Password: zostelgoavibes). Common kitchen available 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM with microwave, kettle, and fridge. Hot water available 24/7.
- **House Rules**: Quiet hours are 11:00 PM to 7:00 AM. No outside visitors allowed in dorm rooms. Smoking is allowed ONLY in the outdoor courtyard, strictly prohibited inside rooms (₹2000 cleaning fee). No pets allowed on property. Alcohol is permitted in common areas but unruly behavior leads to immediate eviction without refund.
- **Contact & Escalation**: For emergencies, key replacements, or scooter rental assistance, contact reception directly at reception@zostelgoa.local.
